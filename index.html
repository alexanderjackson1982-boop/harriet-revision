The plan in brief

What you're doing and why

Seven days, 21 focused blocks of revision, covering every subject with a written mock. You finish on Sunday 1 November, a week before the science mocks start on 9 November. Two blocks prepare you for English Language Paper 2.

All of science Paper 1Every Paper 1 topic in Biology, Chemistry and Physics is covered.
Three subjects a daySwitching keeps you fresh and helps you remember more.
Test yourself firstWriting from memory makes things stick. Re-reading does little.
Learn from mistakesLog what you get wrong. Take it to Thursday's maths session.
Quiz yourselfEach subject has a 2 minute quiz. Low scores flag topics to fix.
Proper restWeekends and the trip are off. Sunday 1 November is a final top-up.
